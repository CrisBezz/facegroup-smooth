FaceGroup Smooth

A browser-based mesh utility for assigning smooth, flat or angle-based shading to facegroups without changing the visible geometry.

  • Smooth: averages normals across connected faces within a group.
  • Flat: assigns an independent normal to each triangle.
  • Auto: smooths across edges only when the face angle is under the group threshold.
  • Hard boundary: prevents normal averaging across the group border.

Exports OBJ with explicit normals and GLB with the rebuilt vertex normals.
